HOW I THINK ABOUT GROWTH

Good products still need a way to travel.

01

Find the right people.

Growth isn't always about reaching more people. It's about finding the communities where the product already makes sense.

02

Give them context.

Distribution works better when the message fits the community instead of forcing everyone through the same generic funnel.

03

Build the loop.

The goal isn't one successful campaign. It's creating systems that make discovery, participation and retention repeatable.

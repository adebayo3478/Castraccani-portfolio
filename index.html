03 / HOW I WORK
01

Find the real bottleneck.

More content is rarely the answer. I start by figuring out whether the problem is awareness, positioning, acquisition, activation, retention or distribution.

02

Go where the users already are.

Communities, creators, builders, partners and niche networks usually beat broad, undirected reach.

03

Ship, measure, adjust.

I prefer small experiments with clear feedback over long strategies that never make it into the market.
